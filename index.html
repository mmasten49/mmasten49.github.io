Building With AI

The clearest growth I've had this year is learning to build with AI instead of just prompting it. Three systems that came out of it, all at Alloy.

  • 01A founder-screening system, in use today. I took 79 of Alloy's real QCA Ventures screening decisions and turned them into a calibrated scoring rubric, then a ChatGPT Project, then a live tool that reviews a new application and drafts a recommendation for staff. It recommends. Staff make every decision — that boundary was the design, not a limitation.
  • 02Content rebuilt for how AI actually reads it. Seven articles written from scratch for Alloy's blog, plus SEO and readability rebuilds across 15+ existing ones — restructured into extractable, context-independent sections so answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity can cite them directly instead of skipping past them.
  • 03Five live tools, no engineering background. I designed and shipped five working web tools on Base44 by writing the spec, supplying the data, directing the build, and testing the output myself. Alloy's in-building and remote members use them — and when Alloy has to turn a founder down, staff point them to these instead of sending them away with nothing.
